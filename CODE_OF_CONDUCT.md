# Code of Conduct

Kill Line follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone. That applies regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit others' work, and own your mistakes.
- Keep security discussions responsible. Report vulnerabilities privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), and never post working exploits against other people's systems.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing offensive tooling (exploits, credential stealers, evasion techniques) in project spaces.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers privately. Contact the repository owner (@finchygoldtail) on GitHub, or use GitHub's *Report content* option. We review every report promptly and confidentially, and we may respond with a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, Discussions and any other official channel.
